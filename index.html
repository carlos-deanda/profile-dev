VoluntApp for BAMX Guadalajara

An Android app the food bank's staff use to run volunteer activities.

Staff save an activity as a draft with its place, capacity and schedule, then publish it.

The app never lets an activity pass its capacity or puts one person in two activities at once.

Present, late or absent with one tap per person, by hand or by scanning a volunteer's QR card.

Closing attendance adds the hours to each volunteer's profile and to the reports.
